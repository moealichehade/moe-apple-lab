# Smart Photo Manager

**Created by @MoeAliChehade — Mohamad Ali Chehade**

Smart Photo Manager is an Apple Shortcut designed to make organizing and sharing photos easier on iPhone and iPad.

## Features

- **Add Photos to Albums** — choose photos and place them in a selected album.
- **Share Photos** — send selected photos using the Apple Share Sheet.
- **AirDrop Photos** — share photos with nearby compatible Apple devices through the Share Sheet.
- **Save Photos to Files** — save copies of selected photos to a destination in Files.
- **Convert Photo Formats** — convert selected photos into supported image formats.
- **Choose Photos Manually** — select the images you want.
- **Find Photos by Date** — select a custom date range.
- **Today's Photos** — work with photos taken today.
- **Last Seven Days** — find recent photos from the previous seven days.

## How it works

Run Smart Photo Manager, select the action you need, choose photos manually or by time period, and follow the on-screen prompts. Sharing and saving may ask you to confirm the destination.

## Why I created it

I wanted an easier way to find, organize, convert, and share photos without repeatedly navigating different screens. Smart Photo Manager brings common photo tasks together in one straightforward menu.

Designed for everyday photo organization on iPhone and iPad.
