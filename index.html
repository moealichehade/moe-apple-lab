Featured app · Pause Studio

Busy day? Make room for a pause.

When your day is full of meetings, writing, studying, or screen time, it is easy to keep going without noticing how long you have been sitting. Pause Studio gives you a gentle invitation to step away for a moment, move, drink water, or follow comfortable breathing guidance. You choose when reminders appear and how long each pause lasts.

10:00 AM · BreatheFollow a gentle inhale and exhale
12:00 PM · StretchMove your shoulders, neck, and arms
2:00 PM · HydrateHave water and rest your eyes

Free Mac download · Includes installation instructions.

Illustrative schedule: Monday–Friday, 8 AM–4 PM, every 120 minutes. All times, intervals, duration and active days are customizable.

Why small breaks matter

Taking short breaks is not about doing less. It is about creating room to notice what your body needs and return to your activities with care.

01 · Breathing

Slow, comfortable breathing may support a sense of calm and help you respond more thoughtfully during stressful moments. There is no need to force or hold your breath.

02 · Movement

Breaking up long periods of sitting with gentle movement can reduce stiffness and encourage a more comfortable workday. Choose movements that feel safe for your body.

03 · Hydration

Busy schedules can make it easy to overlook drinking water. A simple hydration cue helps you remember this everyday need and gives your eyes a brief change of focus.

Designed around your day

Pause Studio lives in the Mac menu bar. Set your own work hours and repeat interval, rotate between activities, follow a guided countdown, and use Done, Skip, or Snooze when needed. Turn sound on or off, and enable or disable reminders directly from the menu. You remain in control.

This is a general well-being and workday reminder tool, not medical treatment. Its design draws on common self-regulation and movement-break practices; the app itself has not been evaluated in a clinical study. Pause Studio is a practical workday reminder tool, not medical treatment.

Mac, iPhone and iPad

Universal Clipboard Hub + UniversalClipboardfromMac

Search clipboard history on your Mac and retrieve synchronized entries using the companion Apple Shortcut on iPhone and iPad. Set up the Mac app first.

The creator's exact signed Shortcut is preserved; its separate public download is not yet available.

1. Mac installation and Bridge setup

Download and unzip the Mac app.Extract UniversalClipboardHub.zip in Downloads.
Install using Terminal.
cd ~/Downloads/UniversalClipboardHub
bash Install.command
Open Universal Clipboard Hub.Find its icon in the Mac menu bar.
Configure the Bridge.Open iCloud Sync & Privacy, select Automatic, enable Universal Clipboard Bridge (JSON index), choose a history limit, and Save Settings.
Verify iCloud.Check iCloud Drive/Shortcuts/UniversalClipboardHub/BridgeIndex.json, containing a list of clips with readable preview and file fields. Original text lives in related .txt files.

2. Companion Shortcut setup

Use the original Shortcut.UniversalClipboardfromMac.shortcut, created and customized by Mohamad Ali Chehade, is the intended companion; do not use old UUID-based versions.
Import it.After obtaining the signed Shortcut, open the .shortcut file with Apple Shortcuts on Mac and approve import. Alternatively open it on your iPhone or iPad.
Synchronize devices.Use the same Apple Account, enable iCloud Drive and Shortcuts sync where available, and allow time for the shared files to download.
Access your history.Run UniversalClipboardfromMac on iPhone or iPad, then choose Recent Clipboard, Search History, or Pinned Clipboard to preview and copy a clip.
Troubleshooting.If history is missing, check the Mac app is running, Bridge is enabled, and BridgeIndex.json and the referenced .txt files are present. iCloud downloads can be delayed.

Privacy and limitations

Automatic clipboard sync may include sensitive text. Use privacy exclusions and turn synchronization Off or Manual when you don't want to upload clips. Filters cannot identify every secret. The separate MacClipboardArchive automation is independent of this app. The iPhone Shortcut reads shared history when run; it does not continuously capture the iOS clipboard.

Connected thinking

One Apple ecosystem. Many possibilities.

Each project has its own role: Shortcut Menu DIY launches shortcuts, Mission Control+ prepares workspaces, Magic Drop Zone handles files, Clipboard Hub remembers copied text, Device Orchestra prepares device handoffs, Automation Studio sequences actions, Device Health Station monitors the Mac, and Pause Studio supports short workday breaks.

These are separate utilities today. Direct integration between the apps is a future development goal, not an existing feature.
