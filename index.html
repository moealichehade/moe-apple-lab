APPLE SHORTCUTS · CLIPBOARD MANAGER

Copy it. Save it. Find it. Reuse it.

Clipboard Manager is a standalone Apple Shortcut created by Mohamad Ali Chehade (Moe) · @MoeAliChehade. Save copied text and bring it back later from one simple menu. It runs through the Apple Shortcuts app on iPhone and iPad and is separate from Universal Clipboard Hub.

Install Clipboard Manager

Download the original Clipboard Manager.shortcut file when its download becomes available. On iPhone or iPad, open the file in Shortcuts, review the actions and permissions, and confirm Add Shortcut. Run Clipboard Manager in Shortcuts to open its main menu. Allow access to files or the clipboard if prompted.

The original Shortcut is ready; its website download is being added.

1–3 · View, save, retrieve

1. Clipboard MonitorOpen your saved clipboard entries and select an available item.
2. Save ClipboardSave the text that is currently copied to your clipboard.
3. Retrieve ClipboardBrowse saved entries, select one, and copy it back to the clipboard.

4–6 · Organize your history

4. Delete Selected ItemsChoose entries you no longer need and confirm their deletion.
5. Clear HistoryRemove all saved entries after confirmation.
6. Exit Clipboard ManagerClose the Shortcut when you are done.

One menu, simple everyday workflow

After completing an action, return to the main menu or exit. Clipboard Manager uses native Apple Shortcuts menus, lists, conditions and saved data, without requiring the Mac clipboard-history app.

Privacy: Any text you save to history may contain personal information. Review saved entries regularly, avoid storing passwords, and use Delete Selected Items or Clear History when needed.

APPLE SHORTCUTS · READING PROGRESS TRACKER

Read. Reflect. Remember.

Created by Mohamad Ali Chehade (Moe) · @MoeAliChehade. Reading Progress Tracker helps you follow your reading journey, record progress and keep the ideas you want to remember. It tracks one active book at a time using native Apple Shortcuts on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Get Reading Progress Tracker

Install the original Reading Progress Tracker.shortcut in Apple Shortcuts: open the file on iPhone, iPad or Mac, review its actions and requested permissions, and select Add Shortcut. Run it from the Shortcuts app and choose an action from its menu.

The original Shortcut has been received. Its public download is being prepared.

Set up and track your book

1. Add or Change BookEnter the book title, author and total number of pages.
2. Update Current PageAfter a reading session, record the page you have reached.
3. View Reading ProgressCheck how far you have progressed through your current book.
4. Save Reading ReflectionWrite down an idea, thought or lesson you want to remember.

Reflect and finish

5. View ReflectionsReturn to reflections you have saved while reading.
6. Mark Book FinishedRecord that you completed your current book.
7. Clear Current BookClear the current book so you can begin another.

Example: Add a 300-page book, enter page 120 after reading, then open View Reading Progress to check your completion. Save a reflection when an idea stands out.

More than a reading counter

Reading is not only about finishing pages. It's also about understanding, reflecting and remembering what matters. Use this Shortcut to notice what each book teaches you, at your own pace.

Before clearing a book, review your recorded reflections. The Shortcut is intended for one active book at a time, not as a multi-book library.

APPLE SHORTCUTS · CONTINUE ON ANOTHER DEVICE

Start here. Continue there.

Continue on Another Device is an Apple Shortcut created by Mohamad Ali Chehade (Moe) · @MoeAliChehade. It helps you prepare supported content on one Apple device so you can access it from another instead of recreating it manually. It complements Apple's built-in Continuity features rather than replacing them.

Install the Shortcut

Get the original Continue on Another Device.shortcut file, open it in the Apple Shortcuts app, review its actions and approve Add Shortcut. Use the same Apple Account and enable iCloud Drive and Shortcuts syncing on the devices you want to use. Check any requested permissions when running it for the first time.

The original Shortcut is included in the downloadable package being prepared for this page.

1 · Start on one device

Begin writing, collecting notes, or working with supported content on your Mac, iPhone or iPad. Identify the material you want available when you switch devices.

2 · Run Continue on Another Device

Open the Shortcut and use its prompts to prepare the information for the shared workflow. Complete any save or permission prompts it presents.

3 · Switch devices

On the other Apple device, open the same Shortcut or its shared storage location and access the prepared information. Allow iCloud synchronization time to finish if necessary.

4 · Continue your work

Use the transferred information to pick up your task where you stopped, without manually reconstructing the material.

Why it exists

Moving between Apple devices should feel simple. This Shortcut explores a more practical, organized handoff for everyday work. It does not promise to transfer an entire running app session or every document type.

Tip: Try it first with a short, non-sensitive text sample. Confirm the content is accessible on your second device before depending on the handoff for important work.

One workflow. Multiple Apple devices. Less starting over.

Featured app · Pause Studio

Busy day? Make room for a pause.

When your day is full of meetings, writing, studying, or screen time, it is easy to keep going without noticing how long you have been sitting. Pause Studio gives you a gentle invitation to step away for a moment, move, drink water, or follow comfortable breathing guidance. You choose when reminders appear and how long each pause lasts.

10:00 AM · BreatheFollow a gentle inhale and exhale
12:00 PM · StretchMove your shoulders, neck, and arms
2:00 PM · HydrateHave water and rest your eyes

Free Mac download · Includes installation instructions.

Illustrative schedule: Monday–Friday, 8 AM–4 PM, every 120 minutes. All times, intervals, duration and active days are customizable.

Why small breaks matter

Taking short breaks is not about doing less. It is about creating room to notice what your body needs and return to your activities with care.

01 · Breathing

Slow, comfortable breathing may support a sense of calm and help you respond more thoughtfully during stressful moments. There is no need to force or hold your breath.

02 · Movement

Breaking up long periods of sitting with gentle movement can reduce stiffness and encourage a more comfortable workday. Choose movements that feel safe for your body.

03 · Hydration

Busy schedules can make it easy to overlook drinking water. A simple hydration cue helps you remember this everyday need and gives your eyes a brief change of focus.

Designed around your day

Pause Studio lives in the Mac menu bar. Set your own work hours and repeat interval, rotate between activities, follow a guided countdown, and use Done, Skip, or Snooze when needed. Turn sound on or off, and enable or disable reminders directly from the menu. You remain in control.

This is a general well-being and workday reminder tool, not medical treatment. Its design draws on common self-regulation and movement-break practices; the app itself has not been evaluated in a clinical study. Pause Studio is a practical workday reminder tool, not medical treatment.

Mac, iPhone and iPad

Universal Clipboard Hub + UniversalClipboardfromMac

Search clipboard history on your Mac and retrieve synchronized entries using the companion Apple Shortcut on iPhone and iPad. Set up the Mac app first.

The creator's exact signed Shortcut is preserved; its separate public download is not yet available.

1. Mac installation and Bridge setup

Download and unzip the Mac app.Extract UniversalClipboardHub.zip in Downloads.
Install using Terminal.
cd ~/Downloads/UniversalClipboardHub
bash Install.command
Open Universal Clipboard Hub.Find its icon in the Mac menu bar.
Configure the Bridge.Open iCloud Sync & Privacy, select Automatic, enable Universal Clipboard Bridge (JSON index), choose a history limit, and Save Settings.
Verify iCloud.Check iCloud Drive/Shortcuts/UniversalClipboardHub/BridgeIndex.json, containing a list of clips with readable preview and file fields. Original text lives in related .txt files.

2. Companion Shortcut setup

Use the original Shortcut.UniversalClipboardfromMac.shortcut, created and customized by Mohamad Ali Chehade, is the intended companion; do not use old UUID-based versions.
Import it.After obtaining the signed Shortcut, open the .shortcut file with Apple Shortcuts on Mac and approve import. Alternatively open it on your iPhone or iPad.
Synchronize devices.Use the same Apple Account, enable iCloud Drive and Shortcuts sync where available, and allow time for the shared files to download.
Access your history.Run UniversalClipboardfromMac on iPhone or iPad, then choose Recent Clipboard, Search History, or Pinned Clipboard to preview and copy a clip.
Troubleshooting.If history is missing, check the Mac app is running, Bridge is enabled, and BridgeIndex.json and the referenced .txt files are present. iCloud downloads can be delayed.

Privacy and limitations

Automatic clipboard sync may include sensitive text. Use privacy exclusions and turn synchronization Off or Manual when you don't want to upload clips. Filters cannot identify every secret. The separate MacClipboardArchive automation is independent of this app. The iPhone Shortcut reads shared history when run; it does not continuously capture the iOS clipboard.
